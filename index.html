Privacy Policy

App: X VIDEO PLAYER PRO

Effective date: October 25, 2026

1. Introduction

X VIDEO PLAYER PRO provides video and audio playback, a built-in browser, downloading features, a private media vault, and a password vault. This policy explains how information may be handled when you use the app.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

Depending on the features you use, the app may store media files, download history, preferences, browser-related data, and passwords you choose to save. Password vault data is designed to use Android Keystore-backed encryption.

Downloaded files saved in shared device storage may remain after the app is uninstalled. You can delete those files separately.

3. Advertising and Third-Party Services

The app uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display advertisements. Google and its partners may process information such as advertising identifiers, IP address, device information, approximate location inferred from IP address, ad interactions, and diagnostic data, as permitted by applicable settings and law.

Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Browser and Download Features

When you visit websites or request content through the built-in browser or downloader, your device may send requests to those websites. The websites may receive your IP address, cookies, browser information, or other data according to their own practices. Please use these features responsibly and respect the rights and terms of the relevant websites.

5. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to play media, process downloads, operate browser features, save your settings and vault data, display advertisements, and maintain app security and stability.

6. Data Sharing

The developer does not operate a developer-managed server for storing your password vault or downloaded media under the app's current design. However, Google Mobile Ads and websites you use may independently process information as described in their own policies.

7. Permissions

The app may request internet, notification, media or file access, and other Android permissions needed for the features you use. You can manage available permissions in your device settings. Denying a permission may limit the related feature.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Information stored locally remains until you delete it, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app, as applicable. Files saved outside the app's private storage may need to be deleted separately. Third-party data retention is governed by the relevant provider's policies.

9. Security

The app is designed to use Android security features for protecting locally stored vault information. No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure. Keep your device and screen lock protected.

10. Children's Privacy

The app is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. If you have concerns about information processed by a third-party service, please contact that service provider.

11. Your Choices

You can manage app permissions through Android settings and review available advertising privacy choices through Google's settings or consent tools, where available.

12. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when app features, data practices, or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published at this web address.
